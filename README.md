# NR-D3CK

this is a cyberdeck inspired by cyberpunk edgerunners with a ai inspired by capri and other blackwall ai, this isnt a copy of the show as it is completly our own fiction with the model having a custom design and personality all tuned on our own server. our own rendition of bringing a ai contruct to life by giving it, its own hardware. the cyberdeck will have a custom esp32 style board with a sd card attachement used for storage of the ai model and to power and control motors and ultrasonic and tilt switch once its powered on the ai will come to life


# what is it

NR is a cyberdeck that will bring ai closer to feeling alive with how a person can interact with it with custom animations and a custom designed case that is almost containing it
# why

we made it as we have worked with ai servers and other hardware in the past including at competition level, also the way ai is going id rather it not be a killer robot due to being asked for a capital of a country everyday
